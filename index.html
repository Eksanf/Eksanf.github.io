AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS & MACHINE INTELLIGENCE

Eksan Fikat.

Postdoctoral Researcher · Tsinghua University

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University. Previously, I worked as a Research Associate at Great Bay University.

My research focuses on autonomous driving, 3D perception and reinforcement learning, with work spanning LiDAR segmentation, point cloud registration, visual SLAM and motion planning.
